# Tropical Kerala Luxury Villa --- Project README

## 1. Project Overview

This project is a concept for a **modern luxury tropical villa in
Kerala**, designed for a plot of approximately **20--25 cents**.

The design combines:

-   Modern luxury architecture
-   Tropical resort-style landscaping
-   Subtle Kerala architectural character
-   Strong indoor--outdoor connection
-   A large open-to-sky central courtyard
-   Private and family-oriented recreational spaces
-   Extensive greenery and fruit-producing plants

### Core idea

> **A Home Where Nature Becomes a Part of Your Life**

The house should feel like a private resort while remaining practical
and comfortable as a permanent family home.

# 3. Design Philosophy

## Architecture

The visual language should combine:

1.  **Modern luxury**
    -   Clean horizontal lines
    -   Large glass openings
    -   Natural stone
    -   Warm wood
    -   Elegant architectural lighting
2.  **Tropical resort**
    -   Deep shaded verandahs
    -   Outdoor living
    -   Water features
    -   Pools
    -   Dense greenery
    -   Natural ventilation and daylight
3.  **Kerala character**
    -   Subtle pitched roof references where appropriate
    -   Natural materials
    -   Courtyard planning
    -   Shaded transitional spaces
    -   Strong relationship with landscape

Avoid making the house look like a generic glass-and-concrete modern
villa.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Main Requirements

## Bedrooms

-   5 bedrooms
-   Every bedroom has an attached bathroom
-   Bedrooms should have privacy and natural light
-   At least one guest bedroom should be convenient for ground-floor
    access

## Master Suite

The master suite is intended to feel like a private resort zone.

Required elements:

-   Large bedroom
-   Private lounge/sitting area
-   Walk-in wardrobe
-   Luxury bathroom
-   Private plunge pool
-   Private garden
-   Private terrace
-   Outdoor shower

The master zone should feel secluded without becoming disconnected from
the rest of the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Central Courtyard

The courtyard is one of the main architectural features.

It should be:

-   Open to the sky
-   Green and tropical
-   Visually connected to living and circulation areas
-   Designed with separate landscape zones

### Suggested courtyard zones

Rather than mixing everything together, use distinct areas:

-   Natural stone section
-   Lawn/grass section
-   Dense planting section
-   Small water feature
-   Stepping-stone path
-   Seating/relaxation pocket
-   Feature tree or planted focal point

The courtyard should provide daylight, ventilation and a strong visual
identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Main Swimming Pool

The main swimming pool should be located toward the **rear of the
property**.

Desired character:

-   Private
-   Tropical
-   Resort-like
-   Surrounded by greenery
-   Pool deck
-   Poolside seating
-   Outdoor relaxation
-   Optional shaded pavilion/gazebo
-   Visual connection to the house without making the pool visible from
    the road

The main pool should feel like a destination within the property.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Outdoor Living & Landscape

Landscape is a major component of the project.

The property should include many plants, preferably including
fruit-producing trees and useful edible plants.

### Landscape concept

Possible planting categories:

-   Coconut
-   Banana
-   Mango
-   Jackfruit
-   Guava
-   Papaya
-   Citrus
-   Curry leaf
-   Herbs
-   Flowering tropical plants
-   Palms
-   Shade trees
-   Ground cover

Exact species should be finalized later according to site conditions,
sunlight, soil, drainage and local availability.

### Outdoor social spaces

Create several separate destinations rather than one large patio:

-   Evening seating garden
-   Family gathering area
-   Poolside lounge
-   Outdoor dining
-   Quiet reading/relaxation corner
-   Garden pavilion
-   Small fire/feature seating area if practical

The garden should feel explorable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Well

A traditional/functional **well outside the house** is required.

The well should be incorporated into the landscape rather than treated
as a purely functional structure.

Possible treatment:

-   Natural stone surround
-   Small planted garden
-   Safe cover/edge treatment
-   Warm lighting
-   Seating nearby if appropriate

Final location must be determined after considering groundwater,
setbacks, drainage, septic/sewer arrangements and local regulations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Parking

Requirement:

-   2-car parking
-   Preferably toward the rear/side
-   Avoid allowing cars to dominate the main entrance view

The north-facing arrival should remain landscaped and elegant.

A service/vehicle circulation route should be separated from the main
pedestrian experience where possible.

# 12. Home Theatre

Capacity:

**8 people**

Desired features:

-   Comfortable 8-seat arrangement
-   Good acoustic treatment
-   Large screen/projector or premium display
-   Dimmable lighting
-   Darker cinematic interior
-   Compact snack/refreshment storage

Snack storage should accommodate:

-   Nuts
-   Healthy snacks
-   Chocolates
-   Drinks
-   Small serving equipment

A small pantry/cabinet should be integrated near the theatre without
making it look like a kitchen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Travel Memory Room

This should be a **small dedicated room**, not a large office.

Purpose:

A personal travel-memory gallery.

Features:

-   Bookshelf
-   Travel books
-   World map
-   Magnetic area for fridge magnets/souvenirs
-   Photo display
-   Small display shelves
-   Travel objects
-   Compact desk or writing surface if useful
-   Warm ambient lighting

The room should feel personal, collected and interesting rather than
overly formal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Gym / Workout Area

Possible equipment:

-   Treadmill
-   Adjustable dumbbells
-   Bench
-   Yoga/stretching area
-   Functional training equipment

-   Natural light

# 15. Circulation Strategy

The house should have multiple experiences rather than one long
corridor.

### Desired movement

**North road → landscaped entrance → foyer → courtyard → living spaces →
rear garden → pool**

Secondary experiences:

**Bedroom → garden/balcony**

**Gym → outdoor/green view**

**Theatre → snack storage**

**Kitchen → service/utility → outdoor area**

**Master suite → private garden → plunge pool**

# 20. Design Consistency Rules

Every future render should maintain:

-   Same overall house geometry
-   Same floor heights
-   Same window language
-   Same material palette
-   Same courtyard location
-   Same pool locations
-   Same landscape logic
-   Same north-facing site orientation
-   Same room relationships
-   Same architectural character

Do not redesign the entire house when generating an individual room.

Each new image should be treated as another camera/view of the same
house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Material Direction

### Exterior

-   Natural stone
-   Warm wood
-   Off-white / light neutral walls
-   Large glass
-   Dark metal details
-   Natural concrete where appropriate
-   Terracotta or Kerala-inspired elements used selectively

### Interior

-   Warm wood
-   Natural stone
-   Neutral flooring
-   Soft textured fabrics
-   Indoor plants
-   Warm indirect lighting
-   Large openings toward greenery

Avoid excessive glossy finishes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. Lighting

### Day

Use natural daylight as the primary source.

### Evening

Use:

-   Warm architectural lighting
-   Landscape uplights
-   Pathway lights
-   Pool lighting
-   Courtyard accent lighting
-   Soft indirect interior lighting

The night version should feel like a private luxury resort without
becoming excessively bright.

# 24. Important Architectural Principle

The house should not feel like:

> "A large building with some plants around it."

It should feel like:

> **"A tropical garden that happens to contain a beautiful home."**

The courtyard, pools, gardens, outdoor seating, fruit trees and views
should therefore be integrated into the architecture from the beginning.
